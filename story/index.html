What Jev made possible, and what we had to build around it.

Jev returns a probability, not prose. That one fact let us treat a model's judgment like a signal with a measurable noise floor, gate on it, replay it, and prove things about the protocol wrapped around it. None of that is available when the output is a paragraph you have to parse.

A calibrated number is not a deterministic one. The same request returned 0.03, 0.03, 0.03, 0.04, 0.04. So we measured the jitter, set the gate above it, and modelled the rest in TLA+ before writing a line of Rust.

  • Measure the noise floor before you trust a threshold.Identity 0.042, reorder 0.059, cohort 0.073. Three floors, not one. A gate set below the floor decides on noise.
  • Five agents on one prompt are one agent.Identical prompts spread 0.010, inside the floor. Paraphrasing raised it to 0.080 on hard cases. Then we audited the paraphrases and found two that weren't.
  • Ask the decision, not a proxy for it."Does the record state pregnancy?" No, correctly, at 0.02. The record states nothing. The real question was "is pregnancy ruled out?" Jev answered exactly what we asked.
  • Derive the quorum bound; don't copy it.TLC swept 24 configurations: safe iff 2Q > N and Q > 2f. Our first guess was wrong. The sweep corrected it.
  • The ambiguous tier will catch you before it catches the model.We labelled a not-ruled-out pregnancy case "escalate". Jev said "not ruled out" 115 of 120 times and was right. Check the label before blaming the answer.
  • The stability gate is not an answerability check.On a genuinely underdetermined allergy record it escalated 86 times and decided 34, split both ways. Add a separate "can this be answered from the record?" question.
  • Escalation is a success state.Under severe chaos the kernel declined trivial questions rather than guess. The invariant is not "always right". It is "never confidently wrong".
  • Never abort a round on a transport blip.Fail-fast voided 71% of rounds under chaos. Degrade-and-continue voided none, and 275 of 329 degraded rounds under severe chaos still decided soundly.
